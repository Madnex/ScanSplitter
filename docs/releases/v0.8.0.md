# ScanSplitter 0.8.0

This release brings a new default photo detector, v7, that is more accurate on
real album scans. It also adds a portable archive bundle export for Projects.

## More accurate photo detection (v7)

- New default detector **ScanSplitter v7**. Boxes now land on the photo itself,
  excluding white and scalloped print borders, photo corners, tape and the
  album page.
- Fewer false boxes from prints seen through glassine/tissue interleaf sheets.
  Light and faded prints on cream pages are found more reliably.
- Measured on 14 real album scans not used for tuning:
  - box quality: 90.3% (v5: 81.7%)
  - tightly fitted crops (IoU ≥ 0.85): 87.6% (v5: 61.7%)
- v5 remains available under Advanced as "v5 · Previous". Existing projects
  keep their saved detector setting.

**Known limitations**

- An empty glassine sheet, or a photo showing through one, can still get an
  extra box. Delete it during review.
- A light sky that blends into the white print border can be cropped slightly
  short.
- Detection takes about 1.8 s per 1200-dpi album spread, up from about 1.3 s.

## Archive bundle export

- Projects can export an **Archive bundle**: a self-describing ZIP with full
  scans, photo crops, optional processed masters and untouched originals.
- A versioned `archive-manifest.json` records checksums, box geometry and
  metadata. See `docs/specs/archive-bundle-v1.md`.

## Other changes

- The benchmark gains a 94-case robustness suite (rotation, many small prints,
  tight gutters, phone captures, Polaroids, low contrast), plus `--manifest`
  and per-scenario reports in `benchmarks/evaluate.py`.
- Updated dependencies, including Pillow 13, FastAPI 0.144, onnxruntime 1.31,
  React 19.3 and Vite 8.3.
